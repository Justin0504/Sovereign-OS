How a mission is governed

Every stage can say no. Nothing downstream runs until the stage above it approves.

01 · Charter

charter.yaml

Mission, budget, KPIs and which capabilities exist. The single source of truth.

02 · CEO

Plan

Decomposes the goal into a task graph; prunes to budget, re-plans on failure.

03 · CFO

Price & gate

Prices every task. Daily cap, runway, margin floor, session circuit breaker.

04 · Workers

Execute

Run on your keys. High-risk capabilities are leased per task, then revoked.

05 · Judge

Audit

Scores each deliverable against the charter's KPIs with a category rubric.

06 · Ledger

Record

Append-only USD and token accounting, sequence-numbered.

RefusedOver the cap or the budget: stopped before a token is spent.
BlockedCapability not yet earned: TrustScore below its threshold.
Fail closedLow score, or no usable verdict: repaired or rejected, never passed.
ProofEach audit carries a SHA-256 proof hash, including who judged it.
Verdicts update TrustScore → unlocks or revokes capabilities
