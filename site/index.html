The loop

Every action is scoped, approved, verified, and receipted.

A declarative charter defines the mission, the money, and what counts as done. Everything after flows through four gates — no task consumes a dollar or a permission it hasn't earned.

01 · PLAN

Decompose

A goal becomes a dependency-aware task graph, each task mapped to a declared competency and a token budget.

02 · GOVERN

Budget & permission

Each task clears the budget, daily-burn, and margin checks, and runs only under a just-in-time permission that expires when it's done.

03 · DELIVER

Verify the work

Outputs are graded against a category-tuned rubric; code isn't accepted until its tests pass, and a failed task self-repairs before it ships.

04 · SETTLE

Receipt & pay

Every report is sealed with a SHA-256 proof hash in an append-only ledger; settlement runs over Stripe or x402/USDC.

Why teams run it

Governance built into the infrastructure, not bolted on.

Budget control

Per-task ceilings, a daily burn cap, a runway floor, and a session circuit breaker that fast-fails a runaway loop before it drains the account.

Earned-autonomy permissions

Agents start restricted and earn capabilities through a track record. High-risk powers are granted just-in-time, scoped to one task, and auto-revoked.

trust score · JIT leases

Tamper-evident audit

Every deliverable is verified against the charter's success criteria and sealed with a SHA-256 proof hash in an append-only ledger you can verify offline.

proof_hash · append-only

Verified delivery

Analytic rubrics per work type, test-gated coding, and automatic self-repair — a failed task is re-run with the fix before anything reaches a client.

Your keys · your money

You bring the keys. It never holds the funds.

Sovereign-OS runs on your own machine and your own LLM key, and settles — when you choose to — through your own Stripe account or wallet. It is the control plane — budgets, permissions, audit — not a custodian. Your spend bills your key; your revenue lands in your account.

Bring your own keys
LLM, Stripe, and wallet are yours — kept in your environment, used only for your missions.
Runs where you run it
Self-hosted on your machine or your cloud. No data leaves your box; nothing is sent to us.
Open source under MIT
Read every line, fork it, run it forever. No lock-in, no commission on your work.
Quickstart

Running on your machine in about a minute.

Self-hosted and open source. Your keys live in your own environment — nothing leaves your box. Start the governed workspace, then open it in the browser.

01
Bring your keys
Set your own ANTHROPIC_API_KEY — agents run on your account, metered by your charter.
02
Start the workspace
One command brings up the web console and its append-only ledger.
03
Run a mission
Open localhost:8000, state a goal, and watch it clear the four gates.
your machine · ~/sovereign-os
# Docker — the whole workspace
$git clone github.com/Justin0504/Sovereign-OS
$export ANTHROPIC_API_KEY=sk-ant-…
$docker compose up -d redis web
→ console live at http://localhost:8000 ✓
 
# or without Docker
$pip install -e .
$python -m sovereign_os.web.app 8000
